# Advanced mathematical-object laboratories

These eight laboratories belong exclusively to the education module. They read no mutable core graph and write no graph records. Mathematical objects, controls, and visible quantities are generated by explicit models; the island ecology is an interpretation of those objects, not evidence that arbitrary objects are connected in the research atlas.

Entry point: `src/labs/AdvancedLab.tsx`.

```tsx
<AdvancedLab labKey="spectrum" language="zh" onExplore={recordInteraction} />
```

`language` is `zh | en`. `onExplore` records that a control was used, not that a learner understood a theorem. The parent `ObjectLab` selects this entry point for the eight keys below. Each scene contains its own explanation, controls, live quantities, accessible SVG description, and mathematical boundary. No animation starts automatically.

| Key | Actual mathematical object | Operations | What the display means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ideal` | Principal ideals mℤ and nℤ in ℤ | Change m,n from 1 to 12 | Filled points are members within [−24,24]; the ideals themselves are infinite. Sum has generator gcd(m,n); intersection has generator lcm(m,n). |
| `spectrum` | Underlying prime spectrum of ℤ/nℤ, n∈{6,8,12,30} | Choose n and an element f | A point is a prime ideal (p)/(n), p dividing n. A ring selects membership in D(f), namely p not dividing f. The label 𝔽p is its residue field. |
| `sheaf` | Affine sections of the sheaf of continuous real functions | Adjust one slope and intercept; match the sections | U=(−3,1), V=(−1,3), overlap (−1,1). A global section is offered only when both affine functions agree on the whole overlap. |
| `transport` | Two finite probability measures on a line | Translate target positions and redistribute its mass | Bands are a monotone optimal coupling under |x−y| cost; width is proportional to transported mass. Circle labels give masses; the two banks do not add a second geometric dimension to the cost. |
| `heat` | Exact two-mode solution of the periodic heat equation | Change time t≥0 and initial high-frequency amplitude a | u=e^(−t)sin x+a e^(−9t)sin 3x on a circle of period 2π. The dashed line is the initial condition, the solid line the current solution. |
| `spectral` | Finite Dirichlet discrete Laplacian L=tridiag(−1,2,−1) | Choose dimension N and mode k | Vertices are the normalized eigenvector components. Fixed endpoints are zero. The lower line marks all eigenvalues with the selected mode highlighted. |
| `lln` | A seeded simulation of the iid Bernoulli(p) model | Extend N, change p, choose another path, show a pointwise band | Filled/open seeds are 1/0 outcomes; the curve is the running mean. The seed makes comparisons reproducible, not a proof of independence or convergence. |
| `algebraiccurve` | Real zero sets of circles, xy=c, and y²=x³+ax+b | Choose a family and parameters; visit a singular member | Paths are finite samples satisfying the selected polynomial equation. Cubic zero crossings are bracketed and refined. Copper rings identify singular points. |

## Mathematical contracts

**Ideals.** Bézout gives (m)+(n)=(gcd(m,n)), while simultaneous divisibility gives (m)∩(n)=(lcm(m,n)). Inclusion reverses divisibility: mℤ⊆nℤ exactly when n divides m. No statement that all ideals of all rings are principal is made.

**Prime spectra.** All prime ideals of the finite ring ℤ/nℤ are maximal. Hence this finite spectrum has discrete underlying topology: distinct points have no specialization relation. Repeated prime factors change the ring and its nilpotents without adding topological points. The ring is reduced exactly when n is square-free. The laboratory does not identify the ring, sheaf of rings, or scheme with its bare point set.

**Gluing.** The ambient object is the sheaf of continuous real-valued functions; the controllable sections happen to be affine. They are compatible iff their two coefficients agree. Testing two distinct points suffices for affine sections, but not for arbitrary continuous sections. `overlapSupremum` is the supremum on the open overlap, equal to the largest endpoint value of the absolute affine difference on its closure; it is not claimed to be attained inside the open interval.

**Transport.** Input measures have finite positions, nonnegative masses, and equal positive total mass. The algorithm sorts positions and pairs remaining mass in order, preserving both marginals. It computes ∑ mass·|source−target|. For one-dimensional L¹ transport, this monotone plan is optimal; uniqueness is not asserted. Bands can overlap in the bank diagram without representing crossing spatial trajectories or new edges in the mathematical atlas.

**Heat.** Diffusivity is 1; x is periodic modulo 2π. The Fourier modes decay at rates 1 and 9. Energy is exactly π(e^(−2t)+a²e^(−18t)), and mean temperature is zero. The visual curve samples an exact solution; it is not a general PDE solver or a time-discretization proof.

**Spectral theory.** There are N interior entries and boundary values v₀=vₙ₊₁=0. Eigenvalues are λk=2−2cos(kπ/(N+1)); normalized eigenvectors have entries √(2/(N+1))sin(jkπ/(N+1)). The conventional grid factor h⁻² is deliberately absent. Numerical residuals are displayed honestly. Infinite-dimensional operator domains and continuum boundary conditions are not silently replaced by this finite example.

**Law of large numbers.** The generator is deterministic and seeded. Increasing N preserves the previously generated sample prefix; changing p uses the same uniform sequence for comparison. Hoeffding radius is √(log(2/α)/(2n)) with α=.05. It is a probability bound for each fixed sample size, not a simultaneous 95% guarantee over an entire path, and not a proof that any particular finite path has converged. Sample errors need not decrease monotonically.

**Algebraic curves.** The display is the real affine locus, not the complex locus or projective completion. Both axes have the same pixel length per unit, preserving the shape of circles. Circle radius zero produces an isolated real point; xy=0 gives two intersecting axes. The cubic is singular iff 4a³+27b²=0 (floating comparison is only numerical classification; UI parameters lie on a quarter-integer grid). The singular coordinate is 0 for the cusp, otherwise −3b/(2a). Parameterized or polynomial samples are checked against the equation, but drawing a curve does not certify general algebraic geometry.

## Verification

`tests/advanced-models.test.ts` verifies the direction of ideal inclusion, actual sum/intersection membership, prime-power versus square-free spectra, D(0)/D(1), overlap compatibility, transport marginals and cost, heat initial/periodic conditions and its PDE, eigenbasis residuals and orthogonality, seeded sample prefixes, the fixed-n Hoeffding formula, algebraic-curve residuals, equal-axis scaling and singular degeneracies, and non-finite input rejection.

The mathematical model functions are exported from `src/labs/advanced/models.ts`. Tests use Vitest; all controls use native labeled range/select/button/checkbox elements. Plots are SVG with accessible titles and descriptions. Keyboard and mobile checks are performed independently of numerical tests; temporary standalone QA entry points are removed before handoff.

Verification on 2026-10-22: all 18 model/projection tests passed, and TypeScript typechecking passed. Chromium checked all eight laboratories at 1200 px (English) and 390 px (Chinese), with no page errors, horizontal overflow, or SVG text outside the viewport; keyboard arrows changed a parameter in every laboratory. Additional interaction checks verified the equal-aspect circle (displayed width/height exactly 1), circle and hyperbola degeneracies, compatible sheaf gluing, the opt-in Hoeffding band, the 20-trial caption, D(0) in ℤ/8ℤ, and clamping the eigenmode when matrix dimension decreases.

### Mobile reading and nonvisual access

A second review used the actual full application at `http://localhost:4173`, rather than a standalone component. At 320 px, the original SVG labels were only 8.8–10.4 screen pixels; they now render at 12 px. At 390 px they render at approximately 14 px. Actual SVG screen scaling is observed so that narrow desktop columns are covered too: labels at 640/900 px page widths improved from 10.1/9.6 px to 14 px. The diagram controls offer **Enlarge figure / Fit width** whenever the laboratory container is narrow, with an explicitly focusable horizontal scrolling region. Enlarged labels are 18 px. Removing a fixed minimum SVG height eliminates empty letterboxing without reducing the actual drawing scale. Muted text now has approximately 4.99:1 contrast against the pale plot background.

Every laboratory provides an always-visible current-state description, updated politely for assistive technology, plus a native disclosure containing an HTML data table. Tables have captions, column headers and row headers, and can be scrolled horizontally with the keyboard. They provide:

- all visible integer-ideal members and their generators;
- prime ideals, residues, residue fields and basic-open membership;
- affine section values and differences at three overlap points;
- every positive transport flow, the source/target point masses, and individual costs;
- nine periodic heat samples, alongside the exact mean and current modal amplitudes;
- eigenvector components, Lv, and λv for direct numerical comparison;
- the last eight Bernoulli updates, means, and distances from p;
- representative real-curve points and polynomial residuals.

This closes two curriculum gaps: transport marginals can be checked from actual flow values, and the spectral lesson's instruction to compare Lv with λv now has a component-by-component numerical path. The LLN table also exposes nonmonotone finite updates directly. A table or small numerical residual remains an observation within the stated model; it is not promoted to a general mathematical proof.

The shared number formatter explicitly preserves the requested maximum decimal precision and normalizes rounded negative zero. This prevents a locale formatter from silently shortening the exact circle constant 3.0625 to 3.063 or six-decimal transport data to three decimals. The laboratory dispatcher also rejects inherited keys such as `constructor` and `toString`.

The pure curve model now inserts exact singular roots that a uniform sampling grid can miss. For a=−3/4, b=−1/4, the isolated real point (−1/2,0) appears exactly once in both the model and representative-point table. For b=+1/4, both continuous branches contain their shared node (1/2,0). Points outside the plotting window are not inserted. These boundary cases bring the focused test suite to 22 tests.

All 32 combinations of eight laboratories × two languages × two mobile widths (320/390 px) passed the current-state/keyboard/data-table flow: a keyboard parameter change updates the text, Enter toggles enlargement, arrow keys scroll the enlarged diagram, fitting restores the original state, and the data disclosure exposes a labeled table without page overflow or runtime errors. Browser-extension tools were unavailable in this execution context, so this review used the installed Playwright/Chromium path. Native screen-reader speech output and physical touch devices were not directly tested.

## 中文说明

本组八个实验分别对应理想、素谱、层、最优运输、热方程、谱论、大数定律与代数曲线。每个实验均有独立对象、实际计算、可操作参数和范围说明。理想不是通用连线图，素谱不是本征值谱，层的胶合不以单点相交替代重叠一致，有限抛币轨迹不替代概率论证明。

所有实验、模型、测试与说明只写入 `education/`。本体节点、关系、坐标、来源、类型字段和源文质量标记不被这些教学操作修改；教育实验与本体研究图谱可分别演进。
